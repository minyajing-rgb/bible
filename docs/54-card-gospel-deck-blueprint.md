# FROM JOHN TO JESUS · 54-Card Gospel Deck

## Product concept

A standard 54-card playing deck derived from the source-critical **From John to Jesus** archive:

- 52 suited cards: four Gospel tellings × 13 ranked story cards
- Big Joker: **LOVE & LIGHT** · a radiant heart
- Small Joker: **JESUS · A GESTURE OF LOVE** · Jesus making a contemporary finger-heart gesture

The deck is playable, collectible, and usable as a story index. It does not imply that the four Gospels are four fully independent historical votes.

## Suit system

| Suit | Gospel | Narrative character | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| ♠ Spades / 黑桃 | Mark | earliest extant action spine; wilderness, water, conflict, handover | ink black + muted sky blue |
| ♦ Diamonds / 方块 | Matthew | fulfillment, kingdom, teaching, and explicit explanatory additions | ink black + pale gold |
| ♣ Clubs / 梅花 | Luke | social ethics, outsiders, prayer, deeds, and returned testimony | ink black + soft sage |
| ♥ Hearts / 红桃 | John | witness, recognition, light, relationship, and theological reframing | ink black + pale coral |

The suit system is editorial navigation, not a claim of equal independence or identical historical weight.

## Rank grammar

- `A` opens the Gospel's John-to-Jesus world.
- `2–10` advance chronologically through words, actions, and encounter.
- `J` is the first major turn or question.
- `Q` is witness, evaluation, or handover.
- `K` is the Gospel's culminating John-to-Jesus statement.

## Card face

Standard poker size: **63 × 88 mm**, with **3 mm bleed** on all sides.

- Print canvas: 69 × 94 mm; 300 dpi = approximately 815 × 1110 px.
- Bleed: 3 mm on every side. `print/` contains 815 × 1110 px files; `final/`
  contains the trimmed 63 × 88 mm version.
- Trim box: approximately 745 × 1040 px, centered inside the print canvas.
- Safe zone: keep rank, suit, verse, and essential faces visibly inside the trim
  edge. The corner indexes must not touch either the physical edge or the trim line.
- Mirrored rank and suit indexes in top-left and bottom-right.
- Center: one ultra-minimal black-line story scene.
- Bottom: short literal English line + book/verse + source-layer badge.
- Full Greek and version-history record lives at the matching archive URL/QR, not as unreadable microtype on the playing card.
- One accent color per card; no gradients, church architecture, cross watermark, or invented dialogue.

## Card back

Two-way rotational symmetry. A restrained line-drawn sun is the dominant mark on
a warm-white ground. Two small pointed hearts may meet at the center with their
tips overlapping slightly. Black line plus one pale-gold accent only. The back
contains no rank, verse, text, or orientation clue.

## Production and review order

After the Joker/Ace style proof, complete one Gospel A–K sequence at a time:

1. Mark / Spades
2. Matthew / Diamonds
3. Luke / Clubs
4. John / Hearts

Every card receives two content checks before illustration and a third
post-layout visual/source check before publication. The check record lives in
`series/from-john-to-jesus/deck-54/audit/verified-cards.json`.

## Joker rules

### Big Joker · LOVE & LIGHT

- A heart emits a restrained pale-gold glow through black concentric line rays.
- Title: `LOVE & LIGHT`.
- Subtitle: `The light shines · Love remains`.
- This is the deck's symbolic synthesis, not an attributed ancient quotation.

### Small Joker · JESUS · A GESTURE OF LOVE

- Jesus makes a small finger-heart gesture in the approved short-limbed line style.
- Label: `CONTEMPORARY SYMBOLIC PORTRAIT`.
- Do not cite a Gospel verse or present the gesture as a historical Gospel event.

## Source integrity

Every suited card must contain:

- exact Gospel and verse;
- exact speaker or narrator;
- quote type;
- source layer;
- approximate Gospel date;
- archive ID linking to the full Greek and change record.

Cards that represent actual manuscript variants—especially Luke 3:22 and John 1:34—carry a small `VARIANT` mark and link to both readings.
