# 44-Batch Roadmap

Every chapter/batch contains 10 separate 4:5 cards. Every card has one narrative beat, one scene, one quotation, narration, or explicitly labeled source-analysis unit, and a source-status block.

The editorial classification is chronological and source-critical first, thematic second. The five-Part movement is `Witness → Water → Cost and Handover → Way → Light and Love`.

## Non-negotiable identity exclusion

The following do **not** belong to John the Baptist's story: the beloved disciple at the last supper or cross, the other disciple at the empty tomb, the resurrection breakfast, “Behold your mother,” “He saw and believed,” and “Come and dine.” Those require a separate Johannine-world series.

Luke's infancy material may appear only as a clearly labeled `LUKE-ONLY INFANCY TRADITION` appendix. It is never the default opening batch for the John-to-Jesus narrative.
