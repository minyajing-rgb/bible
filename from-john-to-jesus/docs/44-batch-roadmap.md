# 44-Chapter Text-History Roadmap

**Canonical scope:** 44 chapters × 10 separate 4:5 evidence cards = **440 final cards**.

This is a production roadmap, **not a completion claim**. A chapter counts as complete only when its 10 `final/` PNGs, ledger rows, chapter README, contact-sheet/audit, progress index, and portal index agree.

Every final card uses the three-zone system: earliest recoverable Greek + literal English, a sourced minimalist scene, and a dated change record.

## Visual lock

The binding visual reference is `skills/from-john-to-jesus-production/assets/approved-minimal-line-style.png`.

- ultra-simple thin black ink doodle
- almost pure warm-white field
- compact short-limbed rounded adults; dignified, not babyish
- flat black hair/beard; garments use very few lines
- tiny scene, roughly lower/middle third; about 70% negative space
- exactly one low-saturation accent hue per card
- no watercolor, shading, paper texture, realistic anatomy, elaborate architecture, detailed folds, cinematic lighting, border/frame, or generated typography
- generated image = illustration only; exact Greek/English/dates/labels are rendered deterministically afterward

## Identity exclusion

`John` means **John the Baptist**. The beloved disciple at the last supper/cross, the other disciple at the empty tomb, and the resurrection breakfast do not belong to this series. Luke's infancy material is appendix-only, not the canonical opening.

## Separate companion product

The **54-card Gospel playing deck** is a derivative product with its own print dimensions and suit map. It shares the source/identity/style gates but is **not part of the 440 evidence-card count**.
