# Season 01 · Batch 01 — John to the Jordan

**Status:** Canonical · 10/10 fully typeset final cards
**Narrative spine:** Mark 1:4–9
**Later comparison:** Matthew 3:14–15 · Luke 3:21–22 · John 1:32–33

This is the canonical opening batch. It begins with the adult John the Baptist in the wilderness and moves directly toward Jesus' baptism. It then shows how the authors of Matthew, Luke, and the Fourth Gospel reshape the earlier Markan scene.

## Cards

1. John appears in the wilderness — Mark 1:4–6
2. The crowds enter the Jordan — Mark 1:5
3. The stronger one comes — Mark 1:7
4. Water and Spirit — Mark 1:8
5. Jesus comes from Nazareth — Mark 1:9
6. John directly baptizes Jesus — Mark 1:9
7. Matthew adds John's objection — Matthew 3:14
8. Matthew adds Jesus' reply — Matthew 3:15
9. Luke leaves the baptizer unnamed — Luke 3:21–22
10. John reframes baptism as witness — John 1:32–33

## Research boundary

The cards use the earliest recoverable Greek text, not a claim to possess an untouched autograph. Authorial redaction is separated from later scribal variation. The notes therefore say “the author of Matthew/Luke/John,” never inventing a named secret editor.

Greek base text: SBLGNT via The Koine Bible.

## Publication format

Only files in `final/` are publishable cards. Every final card visibly includes the literal English quotation, earliest recoverable Greek excerpt, book and verse, source layer, date, parallel passage, historical status, and the applicable source/redaction/reframing note. Files in `raw/` are internal no-text illustration assets and must never be published as completed cards.
