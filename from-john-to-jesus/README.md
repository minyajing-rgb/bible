# GGC Spirituality Series — From John to Jesus

**The Witness · The Water · The Light**

In this series, **John always means John the Baptist** (`JOHN_BAPTIST`) unless a different identity is explicitly declared in another series.

## Canonical visual lock

The approved line is deliberately simple:

- ultra-simple thin black ink doodle
- almost pure warm-white field
- compact, short-limbed, rounded adults; dignified, never babyish
- flat black hair/beard; plain garments with very few lines
- tiny scene in the middle/lower evidence zone; roughly **70% negative space**
- **exactly one** low-saturation accent hue per card: pale lavender, light gold, sky blue, coral, or green
- no watercolor, pencil shading, gray modeling, realistic anatomy, elaborate architecture, detailed fabric folds, cinematic lighting, generated typography, border, or frame

The image model produces **illustration only**. Greek, literal English, dates, source labels, change history, and GGC brand lock are applied deterministically afterward.

## Historical baseline

The project does not claim access to autograph manuscripts or a text guaranteed to be untouched by transmission. It distinguishes:

1. historical tradition
2. Gospel redaction
3. scribal variant
4. translation history / later interpretation

Greek quotations use the SBL Greek New Testament where applicable and are checked against critical-text/manuscript evidence. Dates are broad scholarly working ranges, not exact composition dates.

## Separate 54-card playing deck

The 54-card Gospel playing deck is a **companion derivative product**, not part of the 440-card archive completion count. It shares the identity/source/style gates but has its own poker-card dimensions, suit mapping, card back, packaging, pouch, guidebook, and print workflow.
