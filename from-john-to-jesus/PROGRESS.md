## Completion gate for each chapter

A chapter moves to `COMPLETE` only when all are true:

- 10 source-checked ledger rows exist
- 10 raw no-text illustrations pass the visual gate
- 10 deterministic three-zone final PNGs exist
- all Greek/English/source/date/change-track fields are readable
- contact sheet passes visual QA
- audit script passes
- chapter README is current
- this progress table and portal index are updated
