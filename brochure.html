Vision · 为什么要做

不是拆毁信仰，
而是让它更真实。

Not to dismantle faith, but to make it more real—by keeping evidence, interpretation, doubt, and devotion visible together.

我想重新走一遍从施洗约翰到耶稣的道路。不是为了寻找一个可以攻击谁的答案，而是想知道：我们今天熟悉的故事，最早是怎样被讲述的？

当马可、马太、路加与约翰以不同方式记住同一事件，那些差异不只是“错误”。它们也让我们看见，每一代人如何面对信仰、权力、怀疑、牺牲、光与爱。

I want to walk again from John the Baptist to Jesus—not to find an answer that attacks anyone, but to ask how the familiar story was first told.

When Mark, Matthew, Luke, and John remember the same event differently, the differences are not merely “errors.” They reveal how communities faced faith, power, doubt, sacrifice, light, and love.

诚实边界
Honest Boundary

新约作者的亲笔原稿均未保存。“目前证据可恢复的最早希腊文本”是一项基于现存见证的重建，不是未经触碰的原件。No New Testament autograph survives. “Earliest recoverable Greek” means a reasoned reconstruction from surviving witnesses—not an untouched original.
