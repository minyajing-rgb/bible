presented as a Gospel quotation or an ancient textual witness. The four Aces
carry readable English excerpts; their Greek source units and source cautions
are recorded in `proof-batch-01-aces.md`.

slightly at the center. Rank and suit indexes are inset from both the trim line
and the physical edge.
