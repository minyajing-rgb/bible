Both Jokers are explicitly marked as contemporary symbolic designs. Neither is
presented as a Gospel quotation or an ancient textual witness. The four Aces
carry readable English excerpts; their Greek source units and source cautions
are recorded in `proof-batch-01-aces.md`.

The common back is now a two-way pale-gold sun. Its two pointed hearts overlap
slightly at the center. Rank and suit indexes are inset from both the trim line
and the physical edge.

3. Matthew / Diamonds A–J: eleven cards complete; Q–K remain
4. Luke / Clubs A: complete; 2–K remain
5. John / Hearts A: complete; 2–K remain
6. Complete-deck print proof, source audit, and packaging

No suited card may be illustrated until both `PASS_1_TEXT` and
`PASS_2_CONTEXT` are recorded. Every rendered asset must then pass a third
post-layout check.

The playing deck is a companion index to the 440-card research archive. It does not replace the full three-zone evidence cards.

## Completion boundary

The box, bag, leaflet, and promotional images are review-ready design proofs.
They are not yet unrestricted manufacturing files: the box needs the chosen
printer's certified dieline, and the bag needs the supplier's printable-area,
fabric-shrinkage, and ink specifications. The leaflet receives its final QR and
release wording only after the public site and all 54 card records pass audit.
