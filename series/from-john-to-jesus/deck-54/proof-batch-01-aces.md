## Visual audit

- John is `JOHN_BAPTIST`, not the apostle or beloved disciple.
- Jesus does not appear on any Ace.
- Luke’s Ace does not depict an unborn John; it depicts Zechariah and the messenger.
- John’s Ace is a theological visualization, not a claimed historical lamp scene.
- Each suit uses one accent: sky blue, pale gold, sage, or pale coral.
- The back is a two-way rotationally symmetric sun and contains no rank or orientation clue.
- Its two pointed hearts overlap slightly at the center.
- All seven assets have paired 815 × 1110 px bleed files and 745 × 1040 px trim files.
- Corner indexes remain visibly inside the trim-safe area.
