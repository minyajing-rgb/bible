A REAL TEXTUAL VARIANT

Luke 3:22 preserves more than one ancient reading.

主流现代校勘本采用“你是我的爱子；我喜悦你”。另一个古老传统读作“你是我的儿子；我今日生你”，呼应诗篇 2:7。哪个更早，学界仍有争论。

CRITICAL TEXT

Σὺ εἶ ὁ υἱός μου ὁ ἀγαπητός…

“You are my Son, the beloved…”
≠
ALTERNATIVE ANCIENT READING

Υἱός μου εἶ σύ, ἐγὼ σήμερον γεγέννηκά σε.

“You are my Son; today I have begotten you.”

What we do not claim: no named individual or council can be securely identified as the author of this variant. The witnesses preserve different readings; priority and motive are debated.
