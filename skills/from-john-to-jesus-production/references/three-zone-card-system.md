# Three-Zone Evidence Card System

Every public card is a compact evidence page. The illustration serves the source record; it is not the whole deliverable.

## Zone A · TEXT · top 28–32%

Required in this reading order:

1. `GGC Spirituality Series · From John to Jesus`
2. `EARLIEST RECOVERABLE GREEK`
3. Book, chapter, and verse
4. Greek excerpt
5. `LITERAL ENGLISH` and a literal rendering
6. speaker or narrator
7. source layer, approximate composition date, and critical edition

Keep quoted speech distinct from narration. Ellipses must be visible when the Greek is excerpted.

## Zone B · SCENE · middle 34–38%

- One sourced action, relationship, empty landscape, or evidence comparison.
- Minimal black line on warm white; one optional low-saturation accent hue, including no accent.
- The scene may not contain generated text.
- John means John the Baptist.
- A source-comparison card may use a deterministic line diagram instead of a historical scene.

## Zone C · CHANGE RECORD · bottom 30–34%

Title the zone `TEXT & VERSION HISTORY`.

Show two to four dated entries along a vertical or horizontal rule. Every entry contains:

- date or date range;
- category: `GOSPEL REDACTION`, `SCRIBAL VARIANT`, or `TRANSLATION HISTORY`;
- witness/edition;
- one short statement of what differs;
- uncertainty marker when disputed.

Also show `EVIDENCE STATUS` and the compact source-note label.

Do not force three categories onto every card. Mark 1:9 can show Gospel redaction only; Luke 3:22 can show an actual manuscript variant; a later translation card can add translation history. Fabricated completeness is worse than a short timeline.

## Legibility gate

At 100% size:

- Greek body is readable;
- literal English is readable;
- all dates, categories, and witnesses are readable;
- the scene does not cross text zones;
- the bottom zone cannot be mistaken for a decorative footer.

At contact-sheet size:

- book/verse, central claim, and change categories remain identifiable.
